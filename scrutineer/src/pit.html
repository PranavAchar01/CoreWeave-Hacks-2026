SCRUTINEER pit board — the car, on your desk, while the loop runs behind it the broadcast ↗

WHAT IT IS

A card that stays on top. The car on it is the agent's harness as it stands right now — wings for retrieval, floor for verification, compound for sampling. Each time the loop decides a generation the car rebuilds in front of you, and a placard says what was kept and what was thrown out.

Hover the card for the board: runs decided, changes kept, the held-out score and what it has gained, the ten components and their levels, and how long it has been going.

KEEP IT IN VIEW

Pop it out. The ↗ on the strip gives it a window of its own that floats above every other app — switch tabs, switch apps, the car keeps turning. Chrome and Edge do this today; elsewhere the card stays on the page.

Drag it. Take the strip and put the card anywhere. It remembers where you left it.

Embed it. One line puts it on any page of yours:

<script src="https://scrutineer-one.vercel.app/pit.js" async></script>

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM

The strip's dot says which. LIVE means a scrutineer watch server answered — on this origin, or on 127.0.0.1:7777 from anywhere — and every lap, score and verdict on the board is the loop's own, as it happens on that machine. The key never leaves it.

REPLAY means no loop is running, so the board replays the recorded season shipped with this page, one generation every 24 seconds, round again when it ends. The numbers are the season's; only the clock is the board's.

RUN IT LIVE

cd scrutineer/loop
export ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-…
uv run scrutineer watch

Then open this page again — the dot turns green — or leave this one open: the card finds the loop on 127.0.0.1:7777 by itself.
